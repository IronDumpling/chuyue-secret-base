# Resonance

**A Single-Interface Sci-Fi Survival Thriller**

In a world silenced by an invisible invasion, you are the last operator. Resonance: The Signal is an atmospheric immersive simulation where sight is a luxury and sound is survival. Trapped in a frozen shelter, you must bridge the gap between isolation and connection using obsolete technology and a remote-controlled rover.

**Genre:** Immersive Sim / Survival Horror / Narrative Puzzle
**Platform:** PC (Web/Desktop)

---

### The Core Loop

1. **The Listener (Radio)**
Tune into the static. Rotate physical knobs on your dashboard to intercept, triangulate, and decode Morse code SOS signals from isolated survivors.
2. **The Navigator (Rover)**
Deploy your rover to the coordinates. The screen is pitch black. You must actively "Ping" to generate a radar outline, revealing terrain and threats for mere seconds.
3. **The Survivor (Stealth)**
Hide your signal. Enemies hunt by detecting energy radiation. Manage your rover’s noise floor—move slowly, scan sparingly, and balance the need to see with the need to remain invisible.

---

### Key Features

* **Blindsight Exploration:** A unique visual style where the world exists only when illuminated by sound. Information is your most valuable resource, and it fades with every second.
* **Tactile Analog Interface:** No abstract menus. Interact with a diegetic workstation featuring realistic oscilloscopes, spectrum analyzers, and frequency knobs.
* **Physics-Based Ecosystem:** Enemies are part of a wave-physics simulation. Detect them by their base noise, distract them with decoys, or destroy them by matching their frequency to trigger a fatal resonance.
* **Roguelite Mechanics:** Your rover runs on "Wave Cores" scavenged from fallen entities. Each core defines your frequency range and abilities. If the rover dies, the core is lost.
